Strategic Advice: Avoid GIFs for This Use Case

For a website scroll-triggered background animation, you should not use a GIF. Here is why:

Pixelation: GIFs have an extremely limited color palette (256 colors). Your logo uses subtle cyan glows and multi-tone blue gradients. A GIF will make these look blocky and pixelated.

Scroll Control: You cannot easily synchronize a GIF with scrolling. The GIF just loops at its own speed, whereas you want the tiles to assemble at the speed the user scrolls.

The Best Approach: Scroll-Driven SVG (Lottie or GSAP)

The industry standard for what you want to achieve is Lottie (JSON-based vector animation) or a JavaScript library like GSAP (GreenSock) that controls vector paths.

Since your logo is geometric, we can treat every modular tile and every breakaway shard as a separate vector path.
